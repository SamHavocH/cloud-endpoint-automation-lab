# Intune Concepts

## Endpoint Inventory

Endpoint inventory is the collected view of devices an organization manages or monitors. Useful inventory includes hostname, assigned user, operating system, serial number, network address, installed software, security settings, and recent check-in time.

## Compliance Policy

A compliance policy defines the minimum expected state for a device. For example, a company may require disk encryption, firewall protection, active antivirus, current patches, and recent management check-ins.

## Device Check-In

Device check-in is the signal that a device has recently contacted the management service. Fresh check-ins give administrators confidence that policy and inventory data are current. Stale check-ins can indicate offline devices, broken management agents, or retired hardware.

## Remediation

Remediation is the process of moving a device from an unhealthy state back to the expected baseline. It can include installing updates, enabling security controls, restarting management agents, or asking a user to reconnect a device.

## Conditional Access

Conditional access is the idea of making access decisions based on risk and context. Device compliance can be one input. For example, access to sensitive cloud apps might require a known, encrypted, recently checked-in device.
